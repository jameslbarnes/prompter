Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I reach my limit?

You'll be notified when you approach your monthly limit. You can upgrade your plan at any time to continue conducting interviews.

Can I change plans anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Do unused interviews roll over?

No, unused interviews reset each month. We recommend choosing a plan that matches your typical monthly usage.

Is the free trial renewable?

No, the free trial includes 3 lifetime interviews and does not renew. Once you've used your 3 interviews, you'll need to choose a paid plan to continue.

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fees. You only pay the monthly subscription fee. Cancel anytime with no penalties.

How do additional interviews work?

You can purchase additional interviews at any time. They never expire and are used after your monthly plan interviews are exhausted. Buy in bulk to save up to 30%.
